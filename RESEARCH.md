1. How does the directive define statutory interest for late payment?

   Answer: Simple interest at a rate equal to the reference rate plus at least eight
   percentage points. For euro countries the reference rate is the ECB's rate for its
   most recent main refinancing operations.

   Source 1, Article 2(6) and Article 2(7)(a).

2. The owner's note says the EU maximum late fee is 25 EUR. Is that right?

   Answer: No. The directive sets no maximum. It gives the business a fixed sum of
   at least EUR 40 as compensation for recovery costs, plus reasonable costs above
   that.

   Source 1, Article 6(1) and 6(3).

3. Does the business have to send a reminder before it is entitled to interest and to
   the fixed compensation sum?

   Answer: No. Both the interest and the fixed sum are due without the necessity of
   a reminder.

   Source 1, Article 3(1) (interest) and Article 6(2) (fixed sum).

4. What is the current ECB reference rate?

   Answer: Not in the sources. They define the reference rate but give no figure. I
   would need the ECB's official main refinancing rate in force on 1 July 2026, which
   applies to the second half of 2026.

   Source 1, Article 2(7)(a) and Article 3(2)(b).
